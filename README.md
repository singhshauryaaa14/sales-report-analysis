Interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

Project Overview

Developed an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, and yearly sales trends. The project includes three report pages: Sales Overview, Product & Profit Analysis, and Interactive Sales Trend Dashboard.

Key Features

KPI Tracking: Displays Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders, and other key performance indicators.

Yearly Sales Trends: Visualizes sales performance across different years.

Category Analysis: Compares sales and profit across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

Product Profitability: Identifies top-performing products and analyzes profit margins by category.

Discount vs. Profit Analysis: Uses a scatter chart to explore relationships between discounts, sales, and profit.

Regional Analysis: Compares sales across different regions and geographical areas.

Interactive Filters: Enables dynamic filtering by product category, region, and order date.

Project Objectives

Monitor key sales and profitability metrics.

Identify yearly sales trends and top-performing categories.

Analyze product-level profitability and discount patterns.

Enable interactive exploration of sales data.

Support data-driven business decision-making.

Key Insights

The dashboards display sales trends from 2010 to 2013, compare category-level performance, and highlight profitable products. The analysis also enables users to explore how discounts and regional performance relate to overall business results.

How to Use
